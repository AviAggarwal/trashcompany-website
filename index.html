      On the ground

      Design. Segregate. Recover. Reuse.

      Six steps from crowd to report.

      Step 1

      Awareness

      People sort when they know what to do. Campaigns and on-site material reach your crowd before they reach a bin.

      Step 2

      Segregation

      Bins and volunteers set up for your venue, then a second sort by hand into 16 material categories, so each one keeps its value.

      Step 3

      Collection

      Each stream stays apart in its own container. What was sorted at the bin is still sorted at the gate.

      Step 4

      Transportation

      Every category travels to the centre that can actually use it.

      Step 5

      Processing

      Organics become compost or energy. Dry material goes to recycling, upcycling or reuse. Residuals get managed and recorded.

      Step 6

      Data collection

      Everything feeds an audit and an impact report, and a sharper forecast for your next event.

      Can you help my event go zero waste?

      That is what we aim for on every project. We forecast, agree the scope with you, run it on the ground and report the result.

      Do you just supply bins and a cleaning crew?

      Bins are one part of it. We plan, run segregation on the ground, send materials to recovery routes, manage residuals and report on all of it.

      Can you report on venues you didn't manage?

      We report only on waste we handled. For the rest of your tour or portfolio, your team gets a reporting template so the data is gathered the same way.

      What do you need from us to start?

      The date, venue, expected crowd, and food and vendor plan. Send what you have. We'll tell you what's missing.

      Do you only work on events?

      Events are where we started. We now also advise organisations on what materials to use and how to segregate their waste, and we work with campuses on wet waste.

      Do you provide event waste management in Jaipur?

      Yes. Jaipur is home. We have run waste management at Sawai Mansingh Stadium for Rajasthan Royals and Punjab Kings, at JECC for TiE Global Summit, and along the route of the AU Jaipur Marathon.

      Do you work outside Jaipur?

      Yes. We work across Rajasthan and North India, and we have delivered stadium waste management at Barsapara Cricket Stadium in Guwahati. Tell us your city and dates and we will tell you what we can set up there.

      What is bulk waste management, and do you handle it?

      Bulk waste is a large amount of waste from one place in a short time, the way a stadium, venue, hotel or campus produces it. We handle it end to end: segregation at source, collection by material, transport, composting and recycling, and a report by weight.

      How do you measure the results?

      Everything we collect is sorted by hand into material categories and weighed. Your report shows how much was collected, composted and recycled, match by match or day by day, and how each figure was calculated.
