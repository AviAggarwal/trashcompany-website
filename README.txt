Trash Company website, ready to upload

Upload everything in this folder to the root of trashcompany.in
(every file in this folder; there are no subfolders).

Before going live:
1. Contact form: get a free access key for info@trashcompany.in at web3forms.com
   and paste it into index.html where it says: var FORM_KEY="";
2. Address: if you have a street address and PIN code, add them to the
   "address" block inside the application/ld+json script near the top of the page,
   exactly as they appear on your Google Business Profile.
3. After upload, submit https://trashcompany.in/sitemap.xml in Google Search Console
   and Bing Webmaster Tools.
